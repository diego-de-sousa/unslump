### Issue Identified
Current manifest.json uses `"purpose": "any maskable"` which is NOT recommended:
- Maskable icons need special safe zone (inner 80% circle)
- Current icons may get clipped when masked to circles
- Best practice: separate icons for "any" and "maskable" purposes

## Notes

### Manifest Configuration Issues (Phase 1.2 Findings)
**Problem:** Combined `"purpose": "any maskable"` is not recommended
- "any" purpose: Icon displays as-is (current icons perfect for this)
- "maskable" purpose: Icon will be cropped to circles/shapes (needs safe zone)
- Current icons lack safe zone padding - logo extends to edges
- Result: Logo will be cropped on Android adaptive icons

**Solution:** Create separate maskable variants
1. Keep icon-192.png and icon-512.png with purpose: "any"
2. Create new maskable-192.png and maskable-512.png with:
   - Logo scaled smaller to fit 80% safe zone circle
   - Additional 15-20% padding on all sides
   - Full-bleed white background (no rounded corners)
3. Update manifest.json with 4 icon entries (2 any + 2 maskable)

**Design Analysis:**
- Current favicon.svg: logo at 105% scale with 4px padding
- Maskable requirement: logo must fit within 40% radius circle
- Gap: Logo extends 20-25px beyond safe zone
- Maskable design: Scale logo to ~70% with centered positioning
